5\. Ways of working

- Where we coordinate: WhatsApp group for daily coordination, updates, and urgent communication.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

- How we track findings (single source of truth): A shared findings register in the team's agreed repository or document will serve as the single source of truth for findings, evidence, severity, status, and remediation.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

- How we review each other's work / PRs: Team members will review each other's findings and reports for accuracy, evidence, clarity, and completeness. Remediation PRs will be reviewed by another team member before submission, following the agreed repository workflow.

&nbsp;

- How we resolve disagreements on severity: The team will compare the evidence, likelihood, affected assets, and business impact. The team will discuss and agree on a severity rating, escalating unresolved disagreements to the engagement lead.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

- Daily check-in time: Daily check-in: 7:00 PM WAT via WhatsApp.&nbsp;

## 6\. Risks to delivery

*What could stop us delivering on time, and what we'll do about it.*

| Risk | What the team can do |
| :---- | :---- |
| Delayed repository or data-room access | Notify the engagement lead early and work on available documentation while waiting |
| Team members have conflicting schedules | Agree on daily check-in times and deadlines for individual contributions |
| Findings are not documented consistently | Use one shared findings register and an agreed format |
| Reports are delayed | Assign owners and internal deadlines before the official deadline |
| Team members disagree on severity | Compare evidence and business impact; escalate unresolved disagreements |
| A critical issue is discovered | Notify the engagement lead and client contact in writing within 24 hours |
